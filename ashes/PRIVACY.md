# Ashes: The Boy and the Giant – Privacy Policy

*Ashes: The Boy and the Giant* ("the game") is a Honakari game.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

## What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (the chapter you reached) and your settings (graphics, difficulty) are saved only on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. Deleting the game deletes them.

## Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
