# Ashes: The Boy and the Giant – Support

A Honakari game for iPhone and iPad.

Need help, found a bug, or stuck on a chapter? Email **honakari0@gmail.com** and say which chapter you are on and which device you use.

## Controls

- **Move:** the pad at the bottom left
- **Jump:** the large button at the bottom right
- **Use (F):** levers, wheels and doors
- **Grab:** hold to pull or push crates
- **Hand (E):** ask the giant to lower or raise his palm
- **Stay (Q):** ask the giant to wait, or to follow again
- **Strike (C):** ask the giant to break a weak wall
- **R:** back to the last checkpoint

## Common questions

**The giant won't help me.** He has to trust you first. Stay close to him and he will soon do more.

**Can I replay a chapter?** Yes. Main menu, then CHAPTERS.

**It runs slowly on my phone.** Pause, then GRAPHICS, and choose LOW.
